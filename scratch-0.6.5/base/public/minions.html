Model notes

Revenue is calculated per production cycle and scaled by your number of minions. Super Compactor output is compacted discretely over the selected collection interval, including intermediate leftovers. Daily profit is the single ranking basis; there is no separate optimizer ranking. Bazaar selling uses the selected sell method after the effective Bazaar tax. “Auto” chooses the best available net sale route per output item.

7-day mode uses the local price history collected by BazaarFlipper. Price coverage and price CV are shown in the detail view so historical reliability is visible directly instead of being compressed into a single generic badge.
